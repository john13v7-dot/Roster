Rules this build enforces

  • Mon–Fri layout, matching your paper roster exactly.
  • Four shifts: 07:30–16:30, 08:00–17:00, 08:30–17:30, 09:00–18:00. Each rotating person steps to the next shift week over week; fairness wins when that clashes with the simple rotation.
  • Jason and Shehnaz are always complementary — one on 07:30 means the other on 09:00 — and never away at the same time.
  • Opening cover: Jason-or-Shehnaz plus 2 more staff, 3 in total, every day.
  • Closing cover: Jason-or-Shehnaz plus 2 more staff, 3 in total. If neither is closing, Priscilla covers it automatically on the days her own hours reach closing time.
  • Sue, Laura, Megan and Priscilla keep their own manually-set hours.
  • The vacant seat (row 3) shows with no shift and isn't counted toward cover.
  • Holiday and Maternity Leave are picked up automatically for the dates you give them.
  • Joiners and leavers: a new staff member's Start date (set when you Add Staff) means they're not scheduled before it; an End date works the same way for someone leaving.
  • Floor balance — everyone's on one list for now (no ground floor / 1st floor split yet). The Transfer form records a floor move, but it won't change the schedule until floors are split.
  • Eirini's room — not placed yet.

Everything here is generated straight from the real Python scheduling engine — press Print on the Roster screen for the actual Excel and PDF.
